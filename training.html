Jack Daniels Training System

Our training follows the proven Jack Daniels methodology with four main workout types:

  • Easy (E) - Recovery runs, build aerobic base
  • Threshold (T) - Tempo runs, lactate threshold development
  • Interval (I) - VO2 max development, 3-5 min reps
  • Repetition (R) - Speed/economy, 30sec-2min reps
