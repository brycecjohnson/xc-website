2025 Season Plan

August 29 - November 1 · Jack Daniels Method

Workout Key

  • E = Easy pace + strides (conversational pace)
  • T = Threshold/Tempo (comfortably hard)
  • I = Interval (VO2max pace, ~5K race pace)
  • R = Repetition (speed/economy work)
  • LR = Long Run (steady, comfortable)

Weekly Mileage Guidelines

  • Varsity Boys: 30-45 miles/week
  • Varsity Girls: 25-35 miles/week
  • JV Boys: 20-30 miles/week
  • JV Girls: 15-25 miles/week

Important Reminders

Workout Execution

  • Always complete a proper warm-up (10-15 min easy jog + dynamic stretches)
  • Cool-down after every workout (10 min easy jog + static stretching)
  • Adjust paces for weather (slow down 10-15 sec/mile on hot days)
  • Listen to your body - communicate any pain to coaches

Recovery & Nutrition

  • Aim for 8+ hours of sleep every night
  • Hydrate throughout the day (minimum half your body weight in ounces)
  • Eat within 30 minutes after hard workouts and races
  • Ice baths or cold water immersion after hard efforts

Race Week Protocol

  • Reduce training volume by 30-40%
  • Maintain intensity but reduce repetitions
  • Extra focus on sleep and nutrition
  • Visualize race strategy and stay positive
